# Gonzalo Burgos

**Business · Product · Engineering**

I build operating systems for real businesses: turning commercial, legal and operational requirements into software, workflows, controls and repeatable delivery.

My work sits at the intersection of **business design, product ownership and engineering execution**. I use AI-assisted development intensively, with explicit human control over architecture, risk boundaries, acceptance criteria and release decisions.

I deliberately keep production repositories private when they contain customer data models, financial logic, provider integrations, security boundaries or proprietary operating rules. Public repositories focus on **inspectable engineering methods and executable synthetic reference implementations** rather than sanitized-looking copies of sensitive production code.
